Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who sees it and what your rights are.

خصوصية

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: we collect only what you give us in an enquiry and what we need to deliver a service. We don’t run advertising or analytics cookies, and we don’t sell your data.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Khalif Khalif, trading as Al-Maslak al-Muyassar, of [YOUR BUSINESS ADDRESS]. Contact: hello@almaslakalmuyasar.com.

2. What we collect

  • From the enquiry form: your name, email, WhatsApp number (if you give one), country, the service you’re interested in, whether you’re travelling solo or with family (and the number of children), whether you’re a brother or sister, your arrival timing and Arabic level, your message, and how you’d like us to reply.
  • Our correspondence with you by email or WhatsApp.
  • Payment records — invoice and transaction details from PayPal or a bank transfer. We do not see your card number.
  • Technical data — our hosting provider may log standard server information such as IP addresses, and we use privacy-focused visit statistics (see section 5).

Because our audience is Muslim students, some of what you tell us may indirectly reveal your religious beliefs. We use it only to give you the service you asked for.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and take steps you ask for before booking, and to deliver a service you book (contract).
  • To keep the records the law requires, such as for tax and accounting (legal obligation).
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam (legitimate interests).

4. Who we share it with

  • Cloudflare, which hosts this site and provides anonymous visit statistics.
  • Web3Forms, the service that receives your enquiry-form submission and emails it to us.
  • Our email provider (Google) and WhatsApp, if you ask us to reply there.
  • PayPal, to take payment.
  • Landlords, institutes and drivers — only the details needed to deliver your service, and only with your agreement.
  • Professional advisers or authorities, if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Cookies and third-party services

We do not use advertising cookies. To see which pages are useful we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits in aggregate and is designed not to track you across other sites or build profiles of visitors. If you choose a language other than English, Google’s translation service loads and sets a language cookie (“googtrans”) so your choice is remembered; we only load it when you choose a language. This site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, and links to services such as WhatsApp and Telegram, which have their own privacy policies.

6. International transfers

Some of our providers may process data outside the UK, for example in the United States. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking: we delete them after 12 months. If you book, we keep your details while we deliver the service and then for as long as tax and accounting rules require. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, unless we must keep it by law.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to our use of it, and receive a copy in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent you can withdraw it at any time. Email hello@almaslakalmuyasar.com and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Under 18s

Our services are for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
