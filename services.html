Why this exists

Advice from someone who has been through it

Standard & Premium

What “vetted” actually means

We don’t send you a link and hope. For each housing option we check, in person:

  • Location — distance to the institutes, and noise from main roads
  • Floor level, and whether the lift works
  • Water pressure, taps, sockets, air conditioning and included appliances
  • What the price covers — electricity, water, internet, a water filter
  • A written contract, and a translation you can understand
  • Whether the price is fair compared with similar flats nearby
  • How the landlord handles repairs, and who to call when something breaks
  • Whether other students live in the building

What we don’t do

  • We don’t take or hold your rent, deposit or tuition.
  • We don’t promise that a flat or institute place will be available — availability and prices change.
  • We don’t give legal or immigration advice. We share experience and point you to official sources, because visa rules change.
  • We don’t cover other cities. Our experience is Cairo, primarily Nasr City.
