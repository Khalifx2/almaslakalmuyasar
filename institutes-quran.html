Operating under the supervision of Al-Azhar Al-Sharif — Cairo Azhari Institutes Sector, Qurʾān Affairs Department (Registration No. 5748). Very well known among students in Nasr City with a strong reputation.

Registration happens on-site. The teaching method is portion-based: the teacher assigns a memorisation portion for the session, and the student stays until it's completed. Reasonable exceptions exist — for example, if you have another lesson to get to — but you're expected to return and finish the portion.

Looking for a Private Qurʾān Teacher?

We don't maintain a list of private tutors here — vetting an individual teacher properly (especially for children or sisters) really depends on a direct, trusted recommendation rather than a name on a webpage. Ask in the community WhatsApp and Telegram groups linked on the Community page; students already in Cairo are best placed to recommend someone based on your specific needs, level, and gender preferences.
